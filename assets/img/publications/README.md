# Publication thumbnails

One image per paper, named after its citation key in `assets/data/publications.bib`
(e.g. `r2cc.jpg`), and referenced from that entry with
`image = {assets/img/publications/r2cc.jpg}`.

- Shape: 16:10 landscape; about 640×400 px is plenty (shown at 160×100, sharp on high-DPI screens).
- Format: JPEG for photos and figures, PNG or SVG for diagrams with flat colours. Keep files under ~150 KB.
- Content: the paper's key figure or system diagram works well; images are cropped to fill the frame, so keep the subject centred.

Papers without an `image` show a placeholder with the venue and year.
