Earlier Publications (2015–2022)

Prof. Liu's earlier work (at Boston University, Johns Hopkins, and Carnegie Mellon) spans network telemetry, programmable switches, DDoS defense, and streaming algorithms, including a USENIX FAST Best Paper Award and an ACM STOC "Best of Rest" talk. Notable systems include HeteroSketch and SketchLib (NSDI '22), Jaqen (USENIX Security '21), CocoSketch (SIGCOMM '21), NitroSketch (SIGCOMM '19, deployed in DPDK), DistCache (FAST '19, Best Paper Award), and ASAP (OSDI '18).
