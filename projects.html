R2CC

Modern ML training and inference spanning thousands of GPUs can lose 10–15% of GPU-hours to network faults that trigger job-ending timeouts. R2CC (R2CCL) is a fault-tolerant collective communication library that exploits multi-NIC hardware for rapid connection migration, bandwidth-aware load redistribution, and resilient collective algorithms — keeping training jobs progressing through failures with lossless, low-overhead failover.

Accepted to USENIX NSDI '27. See Publications.
